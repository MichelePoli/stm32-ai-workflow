\section{Abstract}

The deployment of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on resource-constrained embedded systems, often referred to as Edge AI, presents significant challenges due to limited memory, computational power, and energy budgets. The STM32 ecosystem provides powerful tools for this transition, such as STM32CubeMX and X-CUBE-AI, but orchestrating the entire Machine Learning Operations (MLOps) lifecycle—from firmware configuration to model optimization and deployment—remains a complex and manual task.

This thesis presents an Agentic MLOps Orchestration Framework designed to automate and optimize the Edge AI development workflow on STM32 microcontrollers. Leveraging the reasoning capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs) and the state-aware orchestration of LangGraph, the framework implements a multi-agent system capable of managing seven specialized workflows: (1) automated firmware project generation, (2) hardware-aware AI model analysis, (3) seamless software integration, (4) intelligent web-based research for best practices, (5) model customization through "neural network surgery", (6) synthetic data generation for domain adaptation, and (7) automated dataset management.

By integrating these disparate components into a unified, agentic pipeline, the framework reduces the technical barrier for developers and improves the efficiency of deploying optimized neural networks on microcontrollers. Experimental results demonstrate that the framework can autonomously navigate the complexities of the STM32 toolchain, providing reliable code generation and hardware-specific optimizations that bridge the gap between high-level AI research and low-level embedded implementation.
