\chapter{Background}
The most challenging part of this work has been dealing with the orchestration of heterogeneous processes involving large language models, hardware-specific optimization, and automated firmware generation. These systems are considered very promising for bridging the gap between high-level AI research and resource-constrained embedded deployment \cite{su2025daao, hong2024workflowllm}. Achieving a reliable framework requires the seamless integration of diverse components: from Edge AI conversion tools and micro-controller configuration environments to agentic reasoning engines and automated code verification tools.

This background section reviews the foundational concepts and state-of-the-art methodologies that support such an integrated workflow. It begins by covering the basics of Artificial Neural Networks and the constraints of Edge AI systems, particularly within the STM32 ecosystem. It then explores the evolution of Large Language Models and the emerging field of Agentic MLOps. Finally, it surveys tools for workflow orchestration, information retrieval via RAG, and automated validation, which are essential for ensuring the reliability of the proposed framework.
