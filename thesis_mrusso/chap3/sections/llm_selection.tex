\subsection{Language Model Selection and Task-Specific Benchmarking}

The performance and reliability of an agentic workflow are heavily dependent on the reasoning capabilities and specialized training of the underlying Large Language Models (LLMs). In my framework, rather than relying on a single general-purpose model, I adopted a multi-model strategy where different LLMs are assigned to specific nodes based on their strengths.

\subsubsection{Task-Specific Model Assignment}

The rationale for model assignment is driven by the specific computational requirements of each node:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{High-Density Code Generation}: While distilled reasoning models like \textbf{DeepSeek-R1} (7B) offer structured logic, I found that \textbf{GPT-OSS} (20B) provides superior results for generating complex NNI trial scripts. The higher parameter count translates to a broader knowledge of Python libraries and fewer syntax hallucinations.
    
    \item \textbf{Inference Latency vs. Reasoning Tokens}: Empirically, \textbf{GPT-OSS} demonstrated lower wall-clock execution times compared to \textbf{DeepSeek-R1}. This is primarily due to the absence of the "thinking" (Chain-of-Thought) token overhead; while R1 generates hundreds of internal reasoning tokens before outputting code, the 20B model generates the final script directly with high zero-shot accuracy.
    
    \item \textbf{Structured Output and Routing}: The routing nodes require strict JSON compliance. \textbf{Llama3-Groq} (8B) was retained for this role due to its high instruction-following performance in low-latency scenarios.
    
    \item \textbf{Specialized Evaluation}: \textbf{DeepSeek-R1} is reserved for the \textit{LLM-as-a-Judge} role in my evaluation suite. Its ability to articulate reasoning is vital for interpreting quality metrics (e.g., faithfulness, relevancy), where the "thinking" overhead is an acceptable trade-off for explainability.
\end{itemize}

This tiered LLM architecture ensures that the agentic logic remains robust while optimizing the hardware resources of the development server.
